About Top Comet

Top Comet Overseas Employment Agency is an employment agency based in Ahlone Township, Yangon. We support candidates throughout Myanmar and help coordinate recruitment processes between job seekers and overseas employers.

Message from the Founder

A Message to Visitors and Young Leaders

Dear Visitors and Young Leaders,

I would like to extend a warm welcome to everyone visiting the Top Comet Co., Ltd. website, as well as to all the bright young individuals embarking on their journey.

With its advanced technology, high-quality education system, and disciplined work culture, Japan is a land of opportunity and a top destination for young people worldwide. The valuable knowledge and extensive expertise of our experienced instructors will serve as a powerful driving force to empower your personal growth and transform your life.

We established Top Comet Co., Ltd. with a clear mission: to enable talented, highly motivated young individuals from Myanmar to travel and work in Japan through legal, safe, and secure pathways.

As a sending agency, we are firmly committed to:

Full Regulatory Compliance

Adhering strictly to Japanese government laws and regulations, while providing precise, end-to-end support for all documentation and application procedures.

Comprehensive Education & Training

Going beyond Japanese language proficiency to systematically teach Japanese customs, living habits, and workplace etiquette that can be practically applied in daily life.

Continuous Support

Offering ongoing assistance and care just like family from the moment candidates depart Myanmar, upon arrival in Japan, and throughout their stay.

We uphold these principles with deep responsibility for the future of our youth. We pledge to remain the most trusted partner for young people aspiring to fulfill their dreams in Japan, as well as for their families.

For Job Seekers

How the Process Works

Five simple steps from first contact to employment arrangements.

  1. Contact the agency
  2. Register and submit documents
  3. Complete the required checks or tests
  4. Attend interview and preparation activities
  5. Proceed with employment and work-permit arrangements

Requirements, fees, processing times, and employment conditions may vary depending on the employer, destination country, and position. Please contact the agency for current details.
